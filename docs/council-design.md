## 0. HARD CONSTRAINTS (non-negotiable, from constitution + founder correction)
- C1 (§13.1 firewall, PER TIER): agents NEVER originate the pipeline. Every
  council output is advisory/simulative only. Promotion to canonical requires
  HUMAN re-origination. The council is a thinking aid, not a legislature.
- C2 (WORLD = ALIGNMENT ONLY): the world tier aligns/translates between country
  models + the IDEAL-bearing. It does NOT steer, decide, or prescribe. A world
  government is at most a centuries-away DIRECTION (CONSTRAINT Z: no arrival),
  never a current goal or function. The world tier has no coercive capacity.
  ALIGN (make goals legible, surface conflict, simulate trade-offs) ≠ CONVERGE
  (impose a common target). The world tier does the former only.
- C3 (NO implementation yet): this is doc/design. Prototype only after refine.
- C4 (SOLO play, with founder): the council runs in-session WITH the founder,
  agents standing in for "humans." Not a background autonomous process.
  DECISION MODEL: NOW = founder + steward co-decide (not blindly, not dictator,
  not deferring — see the fight, decide yourself). FUTURE (system live) = votes
  decide; founder has no last word. Both hold C1.
- C5 (NO COUNTRY RANKING — founder-identified danger): the transformation-diff
  shows per-bearing STRUCTURAL distance + direction, NEVER an aggregate
  "how ideal / better country" index. Framing any country as "more ideal than
  another" implies a legitimacy hierarchy / national-worth score — FORBIDDEN
  (R4 / power-concentration risk). Each country's ns_alignment is descriptive,
  not a score of national worth.
- C6 (IDEAL DERIVATION IS ONE-WAY — guard on founder's "evolve the ideal"): the
  IDEAL's CONTENT derives from the North Star (constitution), NOT from
  aggregating countries. Countries may feed MECHANISM learnings (how a bearing
  gets expressed structurally) that refine the ideal's EXPRESSION — never its
  bearings, and never via country-vote. Inverting this (countries define the
  ideal) = world-government trap. OPEN until guarded in prototype.
- C7 (DISTANCE METRIC RULE — founder-identified, dangerous to communicate): the
  per-bearing distance between a country's ns_alignment and the IDEAL's is a
  PLANNING/MEASUREMENT TOOL, not a scoreboard. Enforced:
    * INTERNAL/OPERATIONAL only — lives in sandbox; used to plan + measure a
      step (did this proposal move N3 toward the bearing?), NEVER published as a
      country grade.
    * PER-BEARING + DIRECTIONAL — "N3 moved +1, R4 unchanged," never "overall
      65% ideal."
    * NEVER AGGREGATED — a single "how ideal" number = ranking = C5 trap
      (legitimacy hierarchy / national-worth score). Forbidden.
    * PROVISIONAL — numbers are founder-estimated (F4/#13/#18); publishing shaky
      estimates as authority is doubly dangerous. Mark every distance provisional.
  The transformation-diff is the delta primitive; the metric is its per-bearing
  read. Same root weakness as Q3/Q5: trust depends on F4 (member validation).

## 1. LAYERED TIERS (subsidiarity: local stays local, only cross-cutting escalates)
  L0 Neighborhood — concrete local collaboration (co-ops, disputes, resources)
  L1 City / Region — coordination, allocation
  L2 Country      — a REAL PSM instance (DE/CH/IN/ROJ/CN) as the world-model
  L3 World        — IDEAL-bearing model ONLY; alignment/translation surface (C2)

  Descent = context switch IDEAL→real→local via transformation-diff.
  Ascent  = learnings bubble up, translated, can cross to OTHER countries (§3).

## 2. COUNCIL SHAPE PER TIER (adversarial, NOT a chorus of yes-agents)
  Role agents (MAGI/facet style — they DISAGREE by design):
    - N1 guardian  (legitimacy-from-voice)
    - N2 guardian  (honesty over comfort)
    - N3 guardian  (connection across difference)
    - N4 guardian  (calm not addiction)
    - R1..R5 guardians (process rules)
  Simulator agent:
    - takes a proposal, runs it through the tier's PSM instance,
      reports simulated effects (reuses worked-example + equalizer logic)
  Moderator agent:
    - synthesizes GUARDIAN DISAGREEMENT into open questions;
      produces NO verdict (verdict = human)
  ENDPOINT TYPES (all advisory, never a verdict — C1):
    - SIMULATED VOTE: role-agents cast a vote; output "4-1 for X; N3 dissented
      on grounds Y." Shows the fight, not a decision.
    - REWORK-FLAG: high guardian tension + no resolution → "unresolved, rework."
    - OPEN QUESTION: the natural output state when contested.
  Output dossier [SANDBOX]:
    proposal | simulated effects (per tier PSM) | guardian tensions |
    simulated vote | open questions | explicit "needs human re-origination" flag

  WHY ADVERSARIAL: multi-agent "consensus" is a known failure (agents confirm
  each other's hallucinations). Disagreement-by-role is the safety. Visible
  deliberation (the fight + the vote) is the ANTIDOTE to founder-deference (Q1).

## 3. CONTEXT SWITCH + CROSS-COUNTRY LEARNING (the key mechanism)
  Descent (top-down context):
    World(IDEAL) --diff--> Country(real PSM) --diff--> Region --> Neighborhood
    Each step translates bearings into the tier's vocabulary; diff is REVERSIBLE
    + LOGGED (our transformation-diff is the primitive).
  Ascent + cross-pollination (bottom-up learning):
    Neighborhood learning --diff up--> City --> Country(DE) -->
      World(alignment) --> Country(IN) --diff down--> ...
  THE DIFF TRANSFERS MECHANISMS, NOT VALUES (founder + steward agreed, Q3):
    - DOABLE: "Rojava's co-presidency works because it's STRUCTURAL" → translate
      to "what in DE's structure could encode parity?" = mechanism transfer.
    - NOT DOABLE: "DE should care about parity like Rojava" = value transfer.
      The diff CANNOT carry intent. Cross-country learning offers resolution-
      MECHANISMS, never assumes shared substantive goals (per C5/C2: not all
      countries want the same "improvement"; the world tier aligns on mechanism,
      not goal).
  IDEAL-EVOLUTION CAVEAT (C6): country→world learnings may refine the ideal's
    EXPRESSION of bearings (mechanism-level), never the bearings' content, never
    via vote. Inversion risk noted; guard required before prototype.

## 4. HANDOFF (per §13.1, per tier)
  Nothing a council produces enters any pipeline. Founder (later: member body)
  re-origins anything to promote. Council = thinking aid. Dossiers live in
  sandbox/ until adopted. PLAY → LEARNING → pipeline-adaptation PROPOSAL → human
  re-origination (Q6 resolved: play output = a proposal to adapt a pipeline,
  flagged for adoption, never auto-applied).

## 8. Q3 RESOLVED — MECHANISM TRANSFER AS A CONTEXT-DIAL (this session)

  8.1 THE TRAP (decontextualization). A mechanism is not free-standing.
  "Rojava's co-presidency works because it's structural" is true THERE because
  of what surrounds it. Strip that away and "encode parity structurally in DE"
  is not a mechanism — it's a slogan wearing a mechanism's clothes. The diff
  carried the WORD "structural" but left the load-bearing context behind.
  => The clean mechanism/value line was never a line. It is a DIAL: how much
     context rides along with the mechanism.

  8.2 WORKED EXAMPLE (Rojava co-presidency → DE). Context layers behind the
  mechanism "every executive office = two co-equal holders, gender-paired, co-veto":
    L1 STRUCTURAL   : two seats/office, gender-paired, co-veto
    L2 ENABLING     : a body that makes co-holders COOPERATE (Rojava: PYD cadre)
    L3 SOCIAL       : population already practices communal/assembly decisions
    L4 HISTORICAL   : war economy + revolution suspended normal politics
    L5 TELEOLOGICAL : jineolojî — ideology that women's liberation is THE axis
  Turning the dial:
    - Carry L1 only  → PLATITUDE. "DE should add co-veto seats." No enabling
      body → in DE the same structure yields DEADLOCK, not parity. The
      mechanism INVERTS on transplant. This is the trap, live.
    - Carry L1+L2    → REAL LEARNING. "Co-presidency needs an enabling body;
      DE's isn't a cadre party — is it coalition discipline? Mitbestimmung
      (co-determination), which DE ALREADY HAS?" Lands on a DE-native anchor.
    - Carry up to L5 → VALUE SMUGGLING. Imports jineolojî's telos (the WHY).
      The diff smuggled intent back in through context.

  8.3 THE DIAL (the resolution shape):
    too little (L1)       → platitude / inverts on transplant
    enough  (L2, ~L3)     → REAL LEARNING (native-anchor found)
    too much (L4–L5)      → value smuggling (imports the telos)

  8.4 THE TRANSLATION TEST (stopping rule — carry to L2 only, land native).
    A mechanism transfer is REAL (not platitude, not value-smuggling) iff:
    1. it names the ENABLING condition the mechanism needs (not just structure); AND
    2. that condition maps to something the TARGET country already has, or could
       build WITHOUT adopting the source's telos; AND
    3. the moment it requires importing the source's "why it matters" (L5), it
       FAILS → flagged value-transfer → REFUSED.
    In-council: the Simulator runs the transfer; an R-process guardian holds the
    stopping rule; a transfer that can't land by L2 gets a REWORK-FLAG, not a
    proposal.

  8.5 REFUSAL IS A FINDING (founder's key upgrade). A refused transfer is not a
    council failure — it is documented DATA about structural distance:
      LANDS on native anchor → "target has LATENT CAPACITY for this bearing-move"
      REFUSED (no anchor / needs telos) → "this move needs structural change the
        target doesn't yet have — LOGGED, not forced"
    Some transfers are PERMANENTLY refused: when enabling and telos are entangled
    at the SOURCE (jineolojî IS why the cadre party exists), the dial has no clean
    L2 stop. That is an acceptable outcome — the transfer is refused and the
    entanglement itself is the finding. (Answers the open objection from grilling.)

  8.6 LEVERAGE MAP (aggregate output, dodges C5). Run many transfers across
    country pairs; count LANDS vs REFUSES per mechanism. A mechanism that lands
    on native anchors in MANY countries AND moves a bearing = high-leverage. One
    that refuses everywhere = premature or context-locked. Output = a ranking of
    MOVES by leverage, NEVER a ranking of COUNTRIES by worth (C5 preserved).

  8.7 COOPERATION VIA LEGIBLE DIFFERENCE, NOT SIMILARITY (C2 hardened). Rejected
    assumption: "countries move nearer a shared ideal → become alike → cooperate"
    (convergence-as-precondition-for-peace; historically the empire logic). Held
    instead: countries each move along THEIR OWN bearing → get better at making
    differences LEGIBLE (the world tier's ALIGN function) → cooperate DESPITE
    difference. Evidence: EU cooperates across wildly different systems; the most
    similar states often fight hardest. Metaphor: ONE shared star (the bearings),
    MANY different ports (each country's structural form). Shared = direction-sense
    (never arrived-at, CONSTRAINT Z). Different = the port. NOT "each country its
    own star" (that kills the shared reference the align function needs).

  8.8 PERMANENT ASYMMETRY (C6 reaffirmed). Ascent carries mechanism-EXPRESSION
    up, never values, never bearings — not even at full implementation. The
    up/down asymmetry is constitutional and PERMANENT, not a pre-launch
    limitation. Do not expect symmetry to arrive later.

## 9. Q2 RESOLVED — WORLD-TIER OUTPUT: THE CONFLICT DOSSIER + LIFECYCLE (this session)

  9.1 THE ARTIFACT (Conflict Dossier — a legibility tool, NOT a verdict).
  For any cross-country tension the world tier emits:
    1. POSITIONS — each country's stance IN ITS OWN vocabulary, stated so each
       side would RECOGNIZE itself (DE: emissions as N4-calm/duty-to-future;
       CN: growth as N1-legitimacy / lift people from poverty).
    2. THE REAL CONFLICT — where the bearings genuinely collide (not "who's
       right"; "these two readings can't both be maximized in this timeframe").
    3. COST MATRIX — simulated per-party consequences of each option (via PSM,
       marked PROVISIONAL). Neutral numbers, e.g.:
         option │ DE   CN   │ status
         ───────┼───────────┼──────────────────────────
           1    │ +1   -1   │ live trade-off
           2    │  0    5   │ live trade-off (CN-favoring)
           3    │ -4    0   │ Pareto-DOMINATED by option 2
           4    │ +5   -5   │ live trade-off (DE-favoring)
    4. MECHANISM MENU — from Q3's leverage map, in EQUIP-form: ≥3 options
       incl. "do nothing"; each tagged who-it-costs / who-it-helps; explicitly
       NOT ranked, NOT recommended.
    5. NON-VERDICT FLAG — "no recommendation; humans re-originate a choice" (C1).

  9.2 WHY A DOSSIER, NOT A DIFF-VIEWER. A diff-viewer implies a correct target
    state to diff against (convergence smuggled back in). A conflict dossier
    makes difference legible WITHOUT positing a right answer — align, not converge.

  9.3 THE PARETO RULE (what the system MAY and MAY NOT do). The system MAY flag
    Pareto-DOMINATED options (worse for ≥1 party, better for none — option 3
    above): that is pure math, not a value judgment, safe + useful. The system
    MUST NOT rank the legitimately-different live options (1,2,4): choosing among
    them needs a VALUE (how much is DE's gain worth vs CN's cost?) that only
    humans supply. Rule: eliminate the provably-worse; never rank the
    legitimately-different.

  9.4 TRANSPARENCY EQUIPS, NEVER PRESSURES (CHALLENGER Q2-T). Show everything;
    hide nothing (a hidden computation is paternalistic, a trust-bomb on leak,
    and steering-by-omission — fatal in a trust-first platform). BUT transparency
    must serve mutual LEGIBILITY, never PRESSURE. The system shows costs; it
    NEVER labels a choice "selfish" or implies a party must justify deviation
    from a "best" option (that is converge-by-shame — coercion by visibility,
    which crushes legitimate difference, e.g. CN's valid N1 growth-priority).
    Numbers create accountability on their own; the system must not editorialize.
      ✓ "option 3 costs CN -4, DE 0"   ✗ "CN is selfish to refuse option 3"

  9.5 CONFLICT LIFECYCLE (three exits from "not solved yet", none let the system
    steer):
      conflict → CONFLICT DOSSIER → do parties agree?
        YES → humans re-originate the chosen option.
        NO  → DORMANT-CONFLICT REGISTER: parked (not closed, not forced) with
              the cost matrix, WHY it didn't resolve (which bearings collided),
              and TRIGGER conditions to revisit ("if clean-energy cost < X",
              "if CN's N1-reading shifts", "if a new mechanism lands in the
              leverage map", or just periodic re-check). When a trigger fires,
              the conflict RE-SURFACES to humans as newly-tractable (C1 holds).
        MEDIATION (optional) → ONLY on MUTUAL request (both parties agree "we
              want to solve this"). A summoned third party MEDIATES (surfaces
              mechanisms, translates, simulates) — never DECIDES. One-party
              summons are forbidden (would weaponize it as pressure). No standing
              body with resolve-power; it is summoned, not standing; consent is
              the activation key. Whatever it produces, the parties re-originate.

  9.6 UNRESOLVED IS LEGITIMATE (CONSTRAINT Z). Non-agreement is not a system
    failure — it is an honest reading of "these bearings don't align yet." Same
    move as Q3's refusal-as-finding. Time is an ally: contexts change, the cost
    matrix shifts, new mechanisms appear. No forcing, no arrival, just holding
    the direction and revisiting as the world moves.

## 10. STANDING CHALLENGERS (recorded, apply platform-wide)
  Q2-T (transparency): a system that computes and HIDES is paternalistic, a
    trust-bomb on leak, and steering-by-omission. In a trust-first platform the
    only leak-proof design hides nothing → show everything; defuse steering
    through PLURAL, COST-TAGGED, UNRANKED framing, never concealment. And
    transparency must EQUIP (mutual legibility), never PRESSURE (converge-by-
    shame). Founder-originated (the leak insight). Applies beyond the world tier.
  PARETO (metric honesty): the system may eliminate the provably-worse (Pareto-
    dominated), never rank the legitimately-different (value trade-offs). Same
    root as C7: a metric stays honest only while plural, provisional, directional.

## 11. Q2 EXTENSION — MULTI-PARTY CONFLICTS, AFFECTEDNESS & THE RED-LINE FLAG (this session)

  11.1 THE BILATERAL ASSUMPTION WAS TOO NARROW. §9 implicitly assumed 2 countries
    per conflict. Real cross-border conflicts (climate) have NO bystanders — CN's
    emissions affect DE and everyone downwind whether or not they were "parties."
    Fix: correct the DEFINITION of party, NOT the activation rule.

  11.2 REJECTED ACTIVATION TRIGGERS (recorded so future-us doesn't reopen naively):
    - THIRD-PARTY summons (an outsider forces two parties to the table) — COERCION.
    - MAJORITY-of-others summons (N countries compel 2 others to "resolve") — this
      IS the UNO trap: the many impose process on the few. Violates C2 (world tier
      has NO coercive capacity) AND the platform's democracy definition
      (legitimacy-from-voice, NOT majority-wins-substance; process protects
      minorities FROM the majority). REJECTED.
    Rule that holds: VOICE scales with affectedness; POWER-TO-COMPEL stays ZERO
    for everyone. That line is the difference between this platform and a new UNO.

  11.3 AFFECTEDNESS (the correct fix). A country materially affected by a conflict
    is a genuine PARTY: it appears in the dossier, its costs are shown, it has
    VOICE (right to be heard + to REQUEST). Climate → a MULTI-PARTY dossier (DE,
    CN, and everyone downwind, each with costs). Any affected party may ASK to
    join and ASK for mediation. Mediation still activates ONLY by consent of the
    affected parties who want in; any party may still decline to be bound. Being a
    party grants VOICE, never POWER-TO-COMPEL.

  11.4 THE DEEP FORK (recorded, honestly). When an affected party asks to be heard
    and nobody will engage, the honest limit of a non-coercive platform is: it can
    make the injustice fully VISIBLE, but cannot FORCE a remedy. Founder's "inner
    fairness guard" wants an ENFORCER; founder ALSO recognizes (this session) that
    this instinct is dangerous. Both true. The design lives on the SEAM between
    them.

  11.5 REJECTED: THE OBJECTIVE FAIRNESS GUARD / -100 AUTO-ENFORCEMENT. Proposal
    was: mark an "unacceptable" cost (e.g. -100, existential threat) that
    AUTO-creates an enforcing task force. REJECTED, because:
    - WHO sets -100? The SYSTEM would have to decide what is "unacceptable" =
      holding a substantive theory of justice = NOT content-neutral (breaks the
      constitution's process-not-substance line).
    - IT WILL BE GAMED: every party reframes its stake as existential; the
      threshold either becomes meaningless or forces the system to ADJUDICATE
      whose existential claim is real (the substantive judgment again, now armed).
    - AUTO-ENFORCEMENT = C2's coercive capacity, hidden inside a number. The
      danger doesn't leave; it hides in the threshold.
    A system that can FORCE "fairness" is a system that can force ANYTHING it
    later decides is unacceptable — and it will eventually be held by someone
    whose idea of fairness we fear. No objective fairness judge exists; any
    system-held one becomes a weapon.

  11.6 ADOPTED: THE RED-LINE FLAG (separate DETECTING from ENFORCING; the system
    does the first, never the second — that seam is the whole design):
    - Any party may DECLARE a red line: "this option is existential for us"
      (SELF-declared, never system-judged).
    - The dossier marks it prominently as a DECLARED stake, not as fact
      ("Tuvalu declares option 4 existential; undisputed / disputed by X").
    - Effect is on ATTENTION + PROCESS VISIBILITY, never force:
        * a conflict with an UNDISPUTED existential claim CANNOT be quietly
          parked in the Dormant Register — it stays SURFACED (annihilation of one
          party while others refuse must not fade to invisible).
        * others' refusal to engage is LOGGED + visible ("3 parties declined to
          address a declared existential stake") — a PROCESS fact, not substantive
          shaming.
    - Enforcement still requires HUMAN re-origination. System ceiling = "makes it
      impossible to ignore," never "compels." Teeth made of SUNLIGHT, not force.

  11.7 THE HONEST LIMIT (owned, not hidden). This CAN fail a genuinely vulnerable
    party: Tuvalu declares existential, everyone sees it, the world still refuses,
    and the system does nothing but keep the light on. Real, painful. But the
    alternative (a system that CAN force) fails MORE parties, worse, later,
    because whoever captures it inherits the enforcement power. We are choosing
    which failure mode to accept; the sunlight failure is the survivable one.
    The platform's promise: we cannot make people be fair; we can make unfairness
    impossible to hide, bury, or pretend not to have seen.

## 13. C6 RESOLVED — THE IDEAL-EVOLUTION GUARD (ascent-side twin of Q3) [RATIFIED 2026-07-19 — founder + KOMPASS]

  > STATUS: worked autonomously in off-mode (bounded, one cycle), then grilled +

  13.1 THE PROBLEM. C6 says: countries may feed MECHANISM learnings up that refine
    the ideal's EXPRESSION, never its BEARINGS (N1–N4), never via country-vote.
    OPEN because nothing mechanically separated "refines expression" from

  13.2 THE KEY DISTINCTION (three layers of the ideal, only the bottom is mutable

      BEARING (N1–N4)      : the direction itself. IMMUTABLE by ascent. Only ever
                             derived from the constitution/North Star, never from
                             countries. (e.g. N3 "connection across difference")
      EXPRESSION           : how a bearing is STRUCTURALLY encoded — the mechanism
                             vocabulary. MUTABLE by ascent (this is C6's opening).
                             (e.g. "parity can be encoded via co-holding offices" —
                             but see §8.2/§8.4: carried bare to L1 this INVERTS into
                             deadlock in a non-cadre context; the L2 enabling anchor
                             must ride with it. This is an EXPRESSION candidate, not a
                             free-standing mechanism.)
      INSTANCE             : a specific country's form. Never rides up at all
                             (that was Q3's job — mechanism, not value, and only
                             to L2). (e.g. "DE's Mitbestimmung")
    C6's whole game: ascent may enrich the EXPRESSION library, never touch the
    BEARING layer.

  13.3 THE GUARD — THE EXPRESSION TEST (ascent-side mirror of Q3's Translation
    Test). A learning may rise from country → ideal-expression IFF:
    1. it adds or refines a MECHANISM-EXPRESSION of an EXISTING bearing (widens
       the "how", names a new structural way to hold a bearing already in N1–N4); AND
    2. it does NOT add, remove, re-rank, or re-weight any bearing (no new N; no
       "N3 now matters more"; no redefining what N3 covers); AND
    3. it arrives as a MECHANISM (structural, telos-stripped per Q3/§8), never as
       a value/goal ("countries want X more"); AND
    4. it is admitted into the expression library by HUMANS ONLY, via the
       constitutional M-rule / member process (north-star-and-rules.md), NEVER by
       country-vote, NEVER by aggregation, and NEVER agent-originated (C1: agents
       may SIMULATE/FLAG an admission, but a human must re-originate it — the
       council does not edit what the ideal "sounds like"). Countries SUPPLY
       candidate expressions; the constitution ADMITS them. Supply ≠ admit — that
       gap is the firewall, and the gatekeeper is human, not the council.
    Fail any clause → the learning is REFUSED as ideal-input (may still live as a
    country instance; it just doesn't rise to the ideal). Refusal-as-finding again.

  13.4 THE INVERSION ALARM (what the guard is really protecting against). If a
    proposed ascent-learning can only be stated as "the ideal should now VALUE X"
    or "enough countries do X, so X belongs in the ideal" — that is the country-
    defines-ideal inversion (C6 trap / world-government back door). Automatic
    refuse. The tell: it names a WANT/WEIGHT (value) instead of a HOW (mechanism),
    or it justifies itself by COUNT (aggregation) instead of by CONSTITUTION.

  13.5 WHY THIS MIRRORS Q3 (same weakness, same medicine). Q3 guarded DESCENT
    (ideal→country): carry mechanism only, to L2, land native, refuse the rest.
    C6 guards ASCENT (country→ideal): carry mechanism-expression only, admit by
    constitution not vote, refuse value/count. Both rest on the mechanism/value
    line being a DIAL — but note the asymmetry of strictness: ASCENT is the
    TIGHTER direction (telos-stripped, pinned to L1–L2, humans-only admission per
    §13.3 clause 4), while DESCENT (§8.4) may carry up to L2 WITH a native anchor.
    Analogous medicine, stricter gate going up. Both treat refusal as legitimate
    data, not failure.
    The asymmetry is deliberate and PERMANENT (§8.8): expressions flow up; bearings
    never do.

  13.6 RESIDUAL RISK (named, not solved — for founder + member process). Slow
    capture: even "expression-only" enrichment, compounded over generations, could
    drift what a bearing FEELS like it covers (the boiling-frog version of the
    inversion). This is a REAL residual risk, NOT fully solved by C6's structure.
    WHAT IS STRUCTURALLY GUARDED. The per-learning guard is the Expression Test
    (§13.3); refusal is legitimate data, not failure. Countries can SUPPLY
    candidate expressions but cannot ADMIT them — that gap is the firewall
    (§13.3, clause 4), and it is structural, not trust-based. The country→ideal
    channel is therefore closed by design.
    WHAT IS NOT STRUCTURALLY GUARDED. The generational accumulation of admitted
    expressions can still shift what a bearing FEELS like it covers. M4
    (north-star-and-rules.md) does NOT freeze the Bearings — it only forbids
    amending the Rules to freeze the system into a fixed good state or to remove
    the amend-capacity. The prior draft mis-cited M4 as a bearing-freeze; that was
    WRONG (corrected on founder review 2026-07-19). No constitutional clause today
    hard-blocks generational expression-drift.
    WHO MAY CHANGE THE BEARINGS. Humans only, via the constitutional M-rule process
    (never country-vote, never aggregation, never agent-originated). A member-body
    amendment that re-defines a bearing is PERMITTED and Z-compatible — it is a
    human act of re-bearing, distinct from the country-supplied channel C6 guards.
    Stating this explicitly: C6 blocks country→ideal; the human→bearing path is a
    SEPARATE, permitted route and must not be confused with the trapped one.
    GENERATIONAL BACKSTOP (process, not structure). The member process SHOULD
    periodically audit the expression library for bearing-drift — does the
    accumulated "how" quietly imply a changed "what"? Same spirit as §7
    destination-word watch, at the constitutional layer. The audit is now specified
    as the RECURRING GATE in §13.7.4 (Bearing Test re-run on all standing bearings);
    its institution is PROPOSED, pending member process. The one-shot gate is §13.7.3.

## 13.7 THE HUMAN→BEARING RE-RATIFICATION PROTOCOL (closes §13.6 residual; human-side twin of the Expression Test) [PROPOSED — founder + KOMPASS ratification pending]

  > STATUS: drafted by KOMPASS (Nous hy3) to close the OPEN risk named in §13.6.
  > Not yet founder-ratified. Mirrors the §13.3 Expression Test on the human→bearing
  > path. Reads as a proposed constitutional procedure; adoption requires founder
  > (now) / member-body (Phase B+) ratification per M1–M4.

  13.7.1 THE GAP THIS CLOSES. §13.6 names a residual risk that C6's structure does NOT
    cover: even with country→ideal closed (§13.3 clause 4, humans-only admission), and
    even with the per-learning Expression Test in force, the human→bearing re-
    ratification path (§13.6, "WHO MAY CHANGE THE BEARINGS") remains OPEN with no gate.
    A bearing may be re-defined by humans via the M-rule process. If that re-definition
    quietly adopts telos-language — a destination-word slip ("improve," "goal,"
    "target," "arrive") — it inverts N0 / CONSTRAINT Z from inside the constitution, with
    no structural tripwire. This is the §13.6 boiling-frog case, now given a tripwire.

  13.7.2 WHY THE FOUNDER IS NOT EXEMPT. The destination-word slip is a LOGGED RECURRING
    PATTERN: the founder himself exhibits it (see north-star §1 Translation rule; §7
    destination-word watch). The very person who holds the bearings is the one who tends
    to re-express them as destinations. Therefore the gate below applies to ALL bearing
    text — founder-origin, member-origin, agent-surfaced — with NO bearer exempt.
    Exempting the founder would re-install exactly the single-point capture the project
    exists to prevent (BIG PROBLEM #1 / #3). The firewall must bite the hand that writes
    it. (This is also the §11 roast principle applied to the founder's own prose: the
    steward flags the slip here even when it originates with the founder.)

  13.7.3 THE BEARING TEST (human-side twin of §13.3's Expression Test; one-shot gate at
    amendment). A proposed re-definition of any bearing (N1–N4) may enter the M-rule
    ratification pipeline ONLY IF it passes ALL of:
      1. PROCESS-EXPRESSIBLE: the text states a DIRECTION or RELATIONSHIP to hold, not a
         STATE to reach. It must be answerable as "toward what?" not "when are we done?"
      2. TELOS-STRIPPED: the bearing's own wording contains NO destination-word. Any
         "improve / goal / target / utopia / arrive / achieve / fully / complete / finish"
         is caught by the §7 destination-word watch on the literal ratification text
         BEFORE debate; the proposer must re-express it as a bearing (per §1 Translation
         rule) or the proposal cannot be put. The slip cannot ride silently into a ratified
         bearing because the test scores the exact text that gets ratified.
      3. Z-COMPATIBLE: the re-definition does NOT freeze the system into a fixed "good"
         state (M4 sweep). A bearing that implies "once X holds, we have arrived" FAILS.
         The test records a one-line Z-statement with the amendment: "this bearing holds a
         direction; it implies no arrival."
      4. AMEND-CAPACITY PRESERVED: the amendment does not remove the capacity to amend the
         bearings again (M4's second clause). A bearing-lock is automatically void.
    Fail any clause → the proposal is RETURNED to the proposer for re-expression, not
    refused-as-data but refused-as-ratifiable. Same spirit as Refusal-as-finding (§8/§13.3).

  13.7.4 THE RECURRING GATE (closes the boiling-frog, the part §13.6 left OPEN). The one-
    shot Bearing Test at amendment is necessary but not sufficient: generational
    expression-drift (§13.6) can make an OLD bearing's standing wording now read as
    teleological even though it passed at adoption. Therefore the member process MUST
    periodically re-run the Bearing Test (§13.7.3) on ALL currently-ratified bearings —
    the same audit §13.6 flagged, now given teeth. Any bearing that no longer passes is
    flagged for re-ratification or re-expression; it does NOT auto-revert, but it cannot
    sit as silently-teleological canonical text. The audit is PROCESS, not structure, and
    is the standing backstop against slow capture.

    RATIFIED 2026-07-26 (founder + KOMPASS) — TRIGGER CADENCE = OPTION C (fixed floor
    + member-triggered override), with a DEAD-TIME guard against churn:

    (a) FIXED FLOOR — the recurring audit runs on a standing cadence of TWO successive
        member-election cycles (aligned with M5's sequential hurdle). The default
        "periodically" is now pinned: the audit MUST occur at least once per two cycles.

    (b) MEMBER TRIGGER — a supermajority petition of the member body may call an
        off-schedule re-ratification at any time. (No numeric cap on triggers — see (d).)

    (c) DEAD TIME (the churn-guard, founder's concern 2026-07-26). After ANY
        ratification (scheduled or triggered), a mandatory cooling-off of ONE member-
        election cycle OR twelve (12) months — whichever is longer — must elapse before
        a new re-ratification may be triggered. The council only FLAGS an early trigger
        as "rejected by rule" (DETECT-not-ENFORCE); it never decides. This bounds churn
        to a maximum of ~2 re-ratifications per two-cycle window in pathological cases,
        normally 1, while preserving the member override as real teeth.

    (d) WHY NO NUMERIC CAP (explicit). Because the trigger is supermajority-gated, a
        numeric cap would only protect against a supermajority — i.e. let a minority
        entrench by exhausting the trigger budget. That contradicts M6 (no standing
        privilege by origin). The supermajority already filters casual misuse; the
        DEAD TIME filters exhaustion/repetition. Therefore: DEAD TIME bounds churn;
        NO cap is set. The founder's instinct (supermajority prevents misuse) is
        correct and is the load-bearing guard; dead time is the rhythm, not a veto.

  13.7.5 SYMMETRY WITH THE REST OF THE SYSTEM. Three DIAL-guards now form a matched set:
      - DESCENT (ideal→country): Translation Test (§8) — carry mechanism, land native.
      - ASCENT (country→ideal-expression): Expression Test (§13.3) — mechanism only,
        humans-only admission.
      - RE-BEARING (human→bearing): Bearing Test (§13.7) — process-expressible, telos-
        stripped, Z-compatible, amend-capacity preserved.
    All three treat refusal as legitimate data; all three are structural (scored on text,
    not trust); all three put the human as the re-originator, never the agent. The
    human→bearing path is the ONLY route that may change a bearing — and now it is the
    ONLY route that must pass a telos-strip before it does.

  13.7.6 RESIDUAL (honest). The Bearing Test is structural on TEXT but cannot read INTENT:
    a proposer could in principle re-express a destination as a bearing and mean a
    destination. The recurring audit (§13.7.4) is the only defense, and it is a human-
    process defense, not a structural one. This is the same honesty ceiling as §13.6:
    language can be gamed; the backstop is human vigilance + the standing §11 roast
    principle. No clause today fully removes the intent-gap. Flag for member process.
